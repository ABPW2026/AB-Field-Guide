STAIN GUIDE

🌿 Organic growth
Use: Surface-appropriate SH. Apply: Downstream for broad coverage or a compatible pump sprayer for controlled spots. Dwell: Keep wet and monitor.
Risk: Drying or over-dwell can cause residue, streaks, plant injury, corrosion, or coating damage.
🛢️ Oil and grease
Use a concrete-compatible degreaser in a dedicated compatible pump sprayer. Follow the label and agitate only when allowed. SH is not a heavy-oil remover.
🟠 Rust and fertilizer
Use F9 BARC or an approved rust system with dedicated acid-compatible equipment. Follow the exact manufacturer process.
Never use bleach-contaminated equipment.
🛞 Tire marks
Use a compatible rubber-mark remover or degreaser. Test sealed concrete first. Avoid pressure that can scar concrete.
⬜ Efflorescence / minerals
Confirm mineral deposits rather than failing sealer. Use compatible mineral chemistry and exact label timing.
❓ Unknown stain
Stop. Photograph it, water-test in a hidden area, and identify before adding chemistry.

EQUIPMENT TROUBLESHOOTING

Engine will not start
Check fresh fuel, valve, oil, choke, throttle, plug lead, and air filter. Follow the engine manual.
Starts, then dies
Check oil, fresh fuel, tank vent if equipped, choke after warm-up, and accessories.
Low / pulsing / no pressure
Check nozzle, water flow, inlet screen, supply hose, purged air, and leaks.
Bogs with surface cleaner
Switch to wand. Inspect both tips, bar, swivel, hose, and quick connects.
Do not blindly adjust the unloader.
No downstream draw
Use soap nozzle. Check injector direction, pickup tube, filter, fittings, and bucket-test with water.
Leaks / unsafe damage
Stop for fuel leaks, milky pump oil, severe vibration, grinding, or damaged high-pressure hose.

FIELD MANUAL

Dwell control
Work small enough to monitor the treated area. Heat, wind, sun, dark material, and porosity speed drying.
Restaurant grease
Plan runoff first. Remove excess, degrease, agitate if allowed, rinse, and reassess.
Plants and property
Control overspray and runoff. Protect fabrics, furniture, vehicles, hardware, cameras, outlets, glass, paint, and vegetation.
Chemical separation
Label containers. Keep SH and acids separated. Use dedicated sprayers.
Quality control
Inspect for stripes, residue, splash marks, missed edges, and dirty flow lines. Photograph the result.

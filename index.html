STAIN GUIDE

🌿 Organic growth
Use: Surface-appropriate SH solution.
Apply: Downstream for broad coverage; compatible pump sprayer for controlled spots.
Dwell: Keep wet and monitor.
If it dries or sits too long: Residue, streaking, plant injury, metal corrosion, or coating damage.
🛢️ Oil and grease

Use a concrete-compatible degreaser in a dedicated compatible pump sprayer. Follow the product label and agitate only when allowed.

SH is not a true heavy-oil remover. Hot water may be needed.
🟠 Rust and fertilizer

Use F9 BARC or another approved rust-removal system with dedicated acid-compatible equipment. Follow the exact manufacturer process.

Never use bleach-contaminated equipment.
🛞 Tire marks
Use a rubber-mark remover or compatible degreaser. Test sealed concrete first. Avoid extreme pressure that can scar the slab.
⬜ Efflorescence and minerals
Confirm mineral deposits rather than failing sealer. Use a compatible mineral remover and exact label timing. Excess strength may etch masonry or damage mortar.
❓ Unknown stain
Stop. Photograph it, note color and texture, test water in a hidden area, and identify the stain before adding chemistry.

EQUIPMENT TROUBLESHOOTING

Engine will not start
Check fresh fuel, fuel valve, engine oil, choke, throttle, plug lead, and air filter. Follow the engine manual starting sequence.
Starts, then dies
Check oil, fuel, tank vent if equipped, choke position after warm-up, and whether the issue changes with accessories removed.
Low, pulsing, or no pressure
Confirm correct nozzle, adequate water supply, clean inlet screen, straight supply hose, purged air, clean nozzle, and no obvious leaks.
Bogs with surface cleaner
Switch to the wand. Inspect both cleaner tips, bar rotation, swivel, hose, and quick connects.
Do not blindly adjust the unloader.
Surface cleaner does not spin / stripes
Shut down, relieve pressure, inspect matched tips, tip direction, bar movement, swivel, walking speed, and overlap.
No downstream draw
Use the soap nozzle. Check injector direction, pickup tube, filter, fittings, and bucket-test using clean water.
Water, fuel, or oil leak
Shut down and relieve pressure. Stop for fuel leaks, milky pump oil, severe vibration, grinding, or damaged high-pressure hose.

FIELD MANUAL

Dwell control
Work small enough to monitor the entire treated area. Heat, wind, sun, dark material, and porosity speed drying.
Restaurant grease
Plan runoff first. Remove excess, apply compatible degreaser, agitate if allowed, rinse, and reassess.
Plants and property
Control overspray and runoff. Protect fabrics, furniture, vehicles, hardware, cameras, outlets, glass, paint, and vegetation.
Chemical separation
Label every container. Keep SH and acids separated. Use dedicated sprayers for incompatible chemical families.
Quality control
Inspect from two directions for stripes, residue, splash marks, missed edges, and dirty flow lines. Photograph the result.
Hard stop: Current product label and SDS override this guide.

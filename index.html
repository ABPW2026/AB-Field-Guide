STAIN DECISION CENTER

Open a stain, then open the severity. Mixes are starting-point field presets for 12.5% SH. Verify product labels, test a hidden area, and use the least aggressive effective mix.

🌿 Organic growth

Green, black, or brown biological growth, often worse in shade or damp areas.

🟢 Light growth

CHEMS

12.5% SH + compatible surfactant only when its label allows.

SIMPLE MIX

1 gal water + 5 oz SH
Optional surfactant: use label amount.

APPLICATION

Pump sprayer for controlled treatment. Downstream may be used after measuring actual injector draw.

DWELL

Keep wet, monitor continuously, then rinse when growth changes color or label-directed dwell is reached.

Never mix SH with acids or ammonia. Protect vegetation, metal, fabrics, and coatings.
🟡 Moderate growth

SIMPLE MIX

1 gal water + 11 oz SH
Optional surfactant: use label amount.

APPLICATION

Apply evenly in manageable sections. Keep wet and rinse thoroughly.

DWELL

Use the shortest effective label-directed dwell. Reapply a safer mix rather than letting chemistry dry.

Test paint, oxidation-prone siding, sealed surfaces, and delicate vegetation first.
🔴 Heavy growth

SIMPLE MIX

1 gal water + 25 oz SH
Optional surfactant: use label amount.

APPLICATION

Controlled pump-sprayer application only as a starting point. Work small and test first.

DWELL

Keep wet and watch constantly. Rinse immediately if the surface or surroundings change unexpectedly.

Your former 1 gal SH + 1 gal water mix is approximately 6.25% from 12.5% stock. That is much hotter than these presets and can increase damage risk.
🛢️ Oil / grease
🟢 Light

CHEM

Concrete-compatible degreaser.

METHOD

Apply only to the stained area, agitate if label permits, keep wet if directed, then rinse.

🟡 Moderate

Degreaser, controlled agitation, repeat treatment if needed. Plan wastewater control before starting.

🔴 Heavy / absorbed

Hot water or professional recovery may be needed. Set expectations because deep shadows may remain.

Do not rely on SH for heavy petroleum or food grease.
🟠 Rust / fertilizer
Any severity

CHEM

F9 BARC or another surface-approved rust system.

TOOLS

Dedicated acid-compatible pump sprayer.

PROCESS

Follow the current manufacturer procedure for the exact stain and surface.

Never use bleach-contaminated equipment. Protect glass, metal, paint, stone, and coatings.
🛞 Tire marks
Light / Moderate / Heavy

Use a rubber-mark remover or compatible degreaser. Increase agitation or repeat treatment before jumping to extreme pressure. Test sealed concrete first.

⬜ Efflorescence / minerals
Any severity

Confirm mineral deposits rather than failed sealer. Use a compatible mineral-removal system, dedicated equipment, exact label timing, and controlled rinse or neutralization.
